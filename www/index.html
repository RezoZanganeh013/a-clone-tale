Lead your little brother through the rat-infested city to the exit. Rats fear light: your torch slowly burns out, so light the braziers to refill it, and keep Teo inside the light.
Aim: mouse, or touch and drag on the game screen (hold to keep firing). Without manual aim, FIRE / Space locks onto the nearest enemy and leads the shot.
Kill rats to earn XP and level up. Hit guards with stones or stay out of their sight. Progress is saved automatically.
Move: joystick / WASD / arrows. Pause: P. Mute: M.
